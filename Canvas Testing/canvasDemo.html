PaintCanvas demo

Freehand region painter — the reusable module.

Each region is a separate pixel mask. Draw on the canvas to paint the selected region; switch regions to paint another. "Brightness view" swaps the per-region colours for a grayscale driven by a demo valueProvider — that's the hook you'd wire to your SPR signal so shape (mask) and brightness (constants) stay decoupled.
